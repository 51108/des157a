An Evening at the Tavern

Fill in the blanks to find out what happens at the tavern

Your Tale

After a long day on the road, I reached a tavern called The Flabby Mice. I ordered a bowl of stew and asked the innkeeper for a room.

“First,” she said, “are you the plumber we sent for?”

I should have said no. Instead, I asked what the job paid.

She placed a lacrosse stick on the counter and pointed toward the cellar. “Take this. You’ll understand when you get down there.”

Below the tavern, a knight in full armor was barking. His helmet had become wedged between two ceiling beams.

“I was demonstrating a battle maneuver,” he explained.

I used the lacrosse stick to pry him loose. After 200 attempts, he dropped to the floor with a crash that silenced the tavern above.

“Bolocks!” shouted the innkeeper. “Did you break another barrel?”

The knight stood, straightened his armor, and announced that urgent royal business required his presence in Croatia. He left through the back door.

The innkeeper handed me a room key and refilled my bowl.

“The king keeps sending him back.”
